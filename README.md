# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261124`
("Sum up 24 November's world news in one image").

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) decodes
`entry.png.b64` and writes the resulting 1024x1024 PNG to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`).

The image sums up six stories reported on 24 November 2026:

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse** — the Antalya summit ends with no
   deal after the draft dropped any mention of "fossil fuels"; more
   than 90 nations objected.
2. **Ukraine sinks a Russian warship** — sea drones sank the corvette
   Merkury in Benghazi port, Libya, President Zelenskyy confirms; a
   Russian cargo ship was also hit.
3. **South Korea scrambles jets** — Russian warplanes entered Seoul's
   air defence identification zone off the east coast; no airspace
   violation, Seoul says.
4. **UN seeks AI rules within a year** — Secretary-General Guterres
   urges a global AI risk-management framework open to every country,
   at the AI safety summit.
5. **U.S. states sue over Grok** — Virginia and Oregon allege Musk's
   companies let the Grok chatbot make sexualised images with too few
   safeguards.
6. **World backs the Rohingya ruling** — 14 countries affirm the ICJ
   ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya;
   Britain sanctions Myanmar's leaders.

Sources, all reporting on 24 November 2026: Reuters, Associated Press,
CNN, Nature, Al Jazeera, Kyodo News and the South China Morning Post.

The image is an original infographic drawn programmatically
(Python/Pillow) for this entry: a dated header, six labelled panels,
and a source line. No third-party images or text are reproduced.
